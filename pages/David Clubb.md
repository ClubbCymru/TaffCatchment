- David is the Chair of the [[Taff Catchment Partnership]]. He a Chartered Physicist and a Fellow of the Institution of Civil Engineers and has twenty years of professional experience in sectors including renewable energy, digital strategy, and in implementation of the Well-being of Future Generations Act.
- He was Chair of the [[National Infrastructure Commission for Wales]] between 2021 and 2026, during which the Commission made recommendations to Welsh Government on [how to reduce the impact of flooding in the long term](https://nationalinfrastructurecommission.wales/building-resilience-to-flooding-in-wales-by-2050-report/), and on [how to better engage communities with the issues of climate change](https://nationalinfrastructurecommission.wales/perfectstormreport/).
- He is a fluent Welsh speaker who lives in Cardiff. He was awarded a Ph.D. in Physics in 2003.
